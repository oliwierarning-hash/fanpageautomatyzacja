💡 Real cases and solutions

🔹 A client with chronic illnesses had a policy with a pre-existing condition exclusion. If he had died of a previously diagnosed illness, the insurer would not have paid. We found a policy that actually covers him.

🔹 A Belarusian friend who was unemployed could not register as a job seeker, which meant he would have paid thousands of PLN if hospitalized. He now has hospital insurance for 300 PLN a year.

🔹 A friend needed an urgent gastroenterologist visit, but the next NFZ appointment was in a year and a half. We arranged private healthcare instead.

🔹 A client without children had life insurance that paid only on death, with no cover for illness or accidents. We changed it so it protects him.

🔹 A warehouse worker and a waiter had accident insurance that covered only the loss of limbs, not burns, sprains or cuts. We found much better policies for them.

🔹 A client working at heights did not realize his insurance excluded work incidents, although that was why he bought it. We fixed it.
